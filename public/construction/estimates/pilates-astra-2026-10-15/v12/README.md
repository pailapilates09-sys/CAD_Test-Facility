# V12 — buy or delegate

V12 is a responsibility view, reviewed on 20 October 2026. It does not replace the original estimate, accept a contractor split, approve a design or place an order.

`procurement.json` owns only research sources, shared responsibility profiles, work-ID / area-ID assignments and two new, unquantified options. Work names, SI scope quantities, zero values and held status join the shared v9 register during the build. No quoted rate, quantity or amount is independently maintained in the decision assignments.

The two tables group repeated trades under one rule. All 51 original works appear once: 37 in professional packages and 14 in owner-managed purchases/direct hires. Engineer review and specialist installation are separate fields. Owners can still buy materials or hire specialists directly for professional packages, subject to the stated handoffs and design agreement.

The two summary allowances stay unallocated. Carpet and vanity are outside the quoted scope and have unknown purchase quantities. The six held works remain on hold; the zero-quantity glass door is not an order.

Retail examples retain their own product units and access status. In particular, the curtain page contains cached delivery information; its catalogue price does not establish a current delivered quote. NBC 105 and NBC 208 were found on official sources, but full-text retrieval failed; this limitation is visible in the research notes. NBC 207 electrical guidance and the selected ceiling-system guidance support the relevant professional handoffs. No numerical anchorage or service design has been produced.

Each version has one purpose, defined in `../versions.json`. Original/v0–v4 now derive their live content from the same source as v5–v11. V10 and v11 continue sharing one material projection and the existing browser drafts. Dated source exports, old compatibility files, PDF evidence and frozen milestone records are preserved; they are not separate editable current estimates.

The deployment check requires complete ID coverage, exactly two four-column tables, unknown new-option quantities, all six holds and a shared source revision across current views. Automated filtering, source-change propagation, escaping and invalid-register cases are tested. Browser visual QA is unavailable in the current runtime.
