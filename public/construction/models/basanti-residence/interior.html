Room and source basis

Seven named room records are available: three on the ground floor, three on the first floor and one on the second floor. This is a register of recorded rooms, not an inventory of every space in the building. The second-floor record is a store. The upper roof is not an additional occupied storey.

The room rectangles are calculated from written width × depth. Furniture clearances, finishes and quantities are not established by those areas.
