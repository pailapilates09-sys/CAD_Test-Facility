Concrete materials · linked to work volume

The PCC ratio is written in the project PDF; the example dry factor, density, water ratio and bag mass are editable assumptions. No RCC grade is inferred. Total mixing water differs from water to add; aggregate moisture, absorption and other contributions must be known. Curing/cleaning water is not included.
