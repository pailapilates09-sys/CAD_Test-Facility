Quick guide
  1. Views: choose a discipline and floor. For MEP, choose a service. Select a route or member to inspect it. Drag the 3D model to rotate; use Fit all if it leaves view.
  2. BOQ: filter by work, unit or basis, then select an item for measurement evidence. Pending is unknown, not zero. CSV exports keep full precision.
  3. Gantt: choose Building or MEP. Expand a group, select an activity, and follow predecessors or related BOQ. The programmes use separate time bases.
