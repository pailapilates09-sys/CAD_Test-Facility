## Defects corrected

- A blank duration was coerced to zero. It now displays an error and leaves the last valid programme unchanged. Zero must be entered explicitly; fractional and nonfinite durations are rejected.
- Duplicate work IDs, duplicate dependency links and overrides for unknown work could enter scheduling. They now fail before recalculation.
- Invalid tile inputs, fractional coat counts and incomplete scenarios could enter quantities. They now fail before a session model is replaced.
- Missing solid heights could be coerced through arithmetic. They now remain unmeasured or cause the unsupported calculation to be withheld.
- Sloping beams were measured as horizontal prisms. They are now rejected by this orthogonal takeoff method.
- Multiple overlapping slab regions could be counted independently. Potentially overlapping bounding regions now withhold the unsupported union for coordination. This deliberately conservative check is not a general polygon boolean solver.
- Dragging in the vector model could also trigger selection. Click qualification now tracks the entire gesture, including a drag returning to its starting point.
- Vector fallback camera inertia continued across slow projection frames. Direct vector orbiting now stops with the gesture; the WebGL renderer retains damping.
- Context visibility reset the camera, and temporary isolation lost the prior view. Context now preserves it; isolation restores it.
- Gantt labels were shortened or scaled with the entire diagram. Full selectable work labels now stay fixed while the timeline scrolls; zoom changes timeline width, and zero-day work has a milestone symbol.

## Verification contract

Automated regressions cover translated one-metre solid volume, non-manifold/open geometry, malformed meshes, gesture cancellation, invalid duration edits, dependency errors, milestone timing, labels/escaping, scenario validity and unsupported geometry. The IFC receipt in `../basanti-residence/data/ifc-validation.json` records reopened geometry checks; `data/calculation-checks.json` records takeoff coverage. Full tests, a clean production build, linked assets and desktop/phone browser interactions are checked before publication.

Gross exchange volume is a sum of displayed closed meshes, not a boolean union or an approved bill quantity. Net RCC junction allocation remains a separate takeoff calculation. Curve-only and unassigned-thickness elements retain null volume. The full export passes 145 independent solid checks; 12 elements are withheld (eight unassigned-thickness footing outlines, two railing curves and two stair meshes that fail the strict closure check). The stairs retain their explicitly provisional takeoff values; this pass does not silently repair their uncoordinated end profiles. Neither kind of check is structural certification.
