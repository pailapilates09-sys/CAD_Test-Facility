What can we reuse, and what are we creating?

From extracted model to editable design

The Pilates SketchUp page is a custom viewer of an extracted, preserved model. Its 2D view, 3D view and item list share one geometry source. BOQ interpretation is paused at the last reviewed work associations.

This sibling designer creates a new parametric room envelope and new layout records. Tables, chairs, counters and cabinets are generated from dimensions. Cabinet panels and shelves are rebuilt when their parameters change. Door and window openings cut the perimeter wall geometry.

The sofa, lockers and sink marked “PILATES source” are reusable copies of selected optimized meshes. They retain original component IDs and the source-file hash. Moving, rotating or resizing a copy changes this design; resizing scales its appearance, without reconstructing its concealed construction or original modelling history.

You supplied PILATES.skp and PILATES.zip. The native extraction used the SKP inside the ZIP. Supported entities were preserved in JSONL, SQLite and SQL archives; the website uses a selected optimized scene and inspection records. The original file and large archives remain in the preserved Drive collection. Pages serves committed/generated copies, with no live Drive editing connection.

Room concepts and arrangements work here now. A complete building system would add storeys, connected rooms, stairs, typed building elements and an exchange format such as IFC. Structural design, construction specifications and approvals require their own model and professional review.

Learning references: SketchUp component definitions and instances · Parametric modelling concepts. The source dataset supplies geometry, not the SketchUp application’s editing tools.
