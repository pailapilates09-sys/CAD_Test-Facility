# Workspace M2 — Studio theme + fluffy Pip

The workspace now has a clearer visual hierarchy, readable colourful text, persistent light/dark modes and a chubby, fluffy Pip that flies across the screen as visitors scroll and move between pages.

## Included

- Shared Studio styling on all 66 pages, with local Manrope typography, larger headings, readable notes and theme-aware chart labels and markers.
- Light/dark controls that remember the visitor's choice.
- Round mint-and-cream Pip with soft purple wings, rosy cheeks, blinking and gentle breathing. Free flight spans both axes of the viewport, responds to scrolling/navigation and drifts away from the pointer.
- A click-through mascot layer, saved flight position, pause/resume controls, reduced-motion support and suspension in hidden tabs.
- All three primary sections and all nine estimate views, including v8's 51 work detail pages, remain available. CAD, original estimate values, held items, allowances and the current CP176 progress record are preserved.
